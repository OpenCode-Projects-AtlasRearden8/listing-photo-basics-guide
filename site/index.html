Listing Photo Basics: A Reseller’s Guide

A small-business manual for resale listing photos that actually sell — built on a phone, from a corner of a room, on any budget.

Who this is for

You sell used or handmade things online: eBay, Amazon, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, your own storefront. You don’t have a studio, a softbox rig, or a designer. You have a phone, a corner with some light, and a bit of time. That is honestly enough.

The goal of every page in this manual is the same: photos that match reality so precisely that nobody has a reason to open a dispute.

Clear photos do three jobs at once:

  1. Sell the item. Buyers click what they can see, inspect, and trust.
  2. Prevent returns and disputes. If the buyer already saw the exact scuff, the exact smudge, the exact measurement, the item they receive is never a surprise.
  3. Protect your time. Every question a buyer doesn’t have to ask is an inbox you don’t have to answer.

The one-sentence version

Show the item, in real light, flaw and all, filling the frame, next to something that proves its size — then spend two minutes in your editor fixing only what your eye already knew was lying.

How to use it

  • Skim the corner-lab page once to set up your space. Do it once; reuse it forever.
  • Print (or screenshot) the shot-list card for whatever you’re listing today.
  • Run the editing checklist before every upload until it’s muscle memory.
  • Turn to the glossary whenever a term on a marketplace help page confuses you.
