# Per-Marketplace Size Habits

Different platforms, slightly different rules. Below is a plain-English habit sheet for the four places small sellers most often need: **eBay, Amazon, Etsy, and KDP covers.** Specs below are summarized from each platform's public help pages and were current as of late 2026 — where a platform changes its numbers (they rotate now and then), its own help page wins.

## The habit summary

| Platform | Minimum accepted | What you should actually shoot | File size / format | Slot count |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| eBay | 500 px longest side | **1600 px or larger** on the longest side | Up to 12 MB; JPEG, PNG, GIF, TIFF, BMP, WEBP, HEIC, AVIF | Up to 24 photos |
| Amazon | 1,000 px longest side (zoom threshold) | **1,600–2,000 px**; main image pure white (RGB 255,255,255), product filling ~85% of frame | JPEG, PNG, TIFF, GIF | Multiple (varies by category) |
| Etsy | 2,000 px (first photo also needs 635 px or more) | **2,000–3,000 px**, square or landscape (4:3) | Under 1 MB uploads best, JPEG or PNG | Up to 20 photos |
| KDP ebook cover | 1,000 × 625 px | **2,560 × 1,600 px** (h × w), 1.6:1 ratio | Under 50 MB, JPEG or TIFF, RGB color | 1 cover file |

## eBay: the flexible one

- **Shoot at 1600 px or larger on the longest side.** That's what eBay recommends and unlocks zoom; 500 px is only the bare floor.
- **Up to 24 photos free** in most categories — use most of them. More photos means fewer "measurements?" questions and better buyer confidence.
- **Square (1:1) or 16:9 looks best** in eBay's placements; your phone's native 4:3 works fine too.
- **No added text, borders, logos, or watermarks** — eBay's picture policy bans them outright, and watermarks do not protect you from screenshot theft anyway.
- **Actual item only.** Stock photos are banned for used, damaged, or defective items — and "used" describes most resale inventory.
- **What this means for your workflow:** shoot once at full phone resolution, then crop to about square, and export out at 1600–2000 px on the longest side. Don't compress hard.

## Amazon: the strict one

Amazon's requirements are the most specific and enforced of the four; read them twice if Amazon is where you sell:

- **Minimum 1,000 px** on the longest side. Below that, Amazon's zoom doesn't switch on. Habit: 1,600–2,000 px.
- **Main image on pure white background** (RGB 255,255,255). Not "almost white," not light gray. Amazon's main-image scans hand out suppression for near-misses, so white foam board or an editing tool's "remove background → fill with white" is worth doing properly (see [Phone editing without fakery](06-editing.md) for where removing background is allowed vs not).
- **Product should fill 85% or more of the frame** on the main image, with close-to-3:4 (width:height) aspect for most product types.
- **Main image rules:** front view; product only; no text, logos, borders, props, or packaging unless it's part of the sale; no illustrations for physical items.
- **Format:** JPEG, PNG, TIFF, GIF; the file size ceiling is generous (small sellers never hit it), but resolution is the real gate.
- **What this means for your workflow:** the corner lab's white sheet gets you most of the way there; then a straight-out white-background crop (item exactly filling frame, no shadow intrusion) for the main image, plus the honest 3/4 and detail shots — unedited in background — as supporting photos.

## Etsy: the warm one

- **2,000 px minimum in both width and height** is Etsy's recommendation, and sellers recommend hovering at **2,000–3,000 px.** Etsy's storage tops out around 3,000 px a side anyway — bigger isn't better, just bigger.
- **First photo sets the shape of the gallery:** shots should stay square *or* landscape consistently (4:3 such as 3,000 × 2,250 is a common landscape pick).
- **First photo minimum 635 px** — below that, Etsy may rank your listing lower in search.
- **No text overlays or collages on the thumbnail** — Etsy's seller handbook is explicit about it, in part because they look messy on mobile and catalog style search favors plain product shots.
- **Keep files smallish:** under about 1 MB tends to upload more reliably on weak connections; JPEG in sRGB color is a solid default.
- **Up to 20 photos per listing.** Lifestyle or "in the home" shots perform well here in a way that pure-white catalogs don't (see [Backgrounds and scale](04-backgrounds.md) for the styled-background tradeoffs).
- **What this means for your workflow:** shoot a square-set batch anywhere 2,000–3,000 px, first image plain and quiet, later slots carrying lifestyle/scale/flaws.

## KDP covers: the publishing side

If you also publish with Amazon KDP as an author (a very *Asdale Dynamics* kind of side quest), cover images follow their own rules:

- **Ebook marketing cover, ideal:** **2,560 (height) × 1,600 (width) px** — a 1.6:1 height:width ratio. Minimum accepted is 1,000 × 625 px, but below the ideal it renders soft on modern tablets and HD-capable apps.
- **Format:** JPEG or TIFF; **RGB color space** (not CMYK);
- **Keep the file under 50 MB** and don't over-compress: Amazon applies more compression at display.
- **Light covers:** Amazon's own guidance is a thin **3–4 px medium-gray border** so white-on-white covers don't disappear against the white page.
- **No pricing or promo details, no spine, no back-cover elements, no barcodes** on the ebook cover file; title and author should match the KDP listing exactly.
- **Print books are different files entirely:** KDP publishes a full-wrap template (front + spine + back with calculated spine width from your page count and paper type). Use KDP's cover template generator, not a hand-measured file — that's exactly the same lesson as the [ruler habit for scale shots](04-backgrounds.md): trust the tape, or the template, over your eye.
- **What this means for your workflow:** design once at 2,560 × 1,600, flat RGB JPEG. Cover *photography* for KDP only matters if you need a mock-up from a physical proof among your Etsy/eBay-style listings — which is just the regular rules with a book as the product.

## One habit that pays forever

Photos at 2,000 px on the long side fit *all four* platforms with room to spare (KDP print covers aren't photos, but ebook covers need 2,560 tall). Shoot once at full resolution, crop twice, and platform needs stop being a thought.
